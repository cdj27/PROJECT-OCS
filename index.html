The problem: nobody knows where to park until they get there

Gate queues

On Family Day, vehicles back up at the gate, slow vehicle checks and make it harder for security to keep control.

Cars circling inside

Drivers hunting for space block internal roads and raise the risk of vehicle conflicts and pedestrian hazards.

Marshals reacting late

Radio calls and manual updates are slow, so marshals are always one step behind the congestion.

How it works

1

Cameras watch the lots

Affordable off-the-shelf cameras (Tapo C310) look over each car park from an elevated angle.

2

AI reads every bay

Computer vision marks each bay as occupied or empty and sends the result to the app.

3

Everyone sees it live

Officers, marshals and organisers open the map in a browser and direct vehicles where there is space.

What you get

One map, every lot

The full SAFTI MI layout with each lot clearly marked available, full or reserved. No training needed.

Wing contacts in one tap

Each lot shows its wing and who to call, so no one chases people by phone or message.

Reserve lots for guests

Authorised personnel book lots in advance for events, replacing physical signs and preventing double bookings.

Nothing to install and no special equipment for users. If the AI gets a bay wrong, anyone can report it, and last-updated timestamps show when data is stale.

Risks we have planned for

The AI misreads a bay

We train on diverse parking images, test against real footage, allow manual correction, and let users report a wrong status.

A camera view is blocked

Cameras go on elevated mounts with coverage checked during installation.

Poor light or weather

We test in different conditions and use outdoor cameras with night vision.

Wi-Fi drops

We secure reliable coverage, monitor camera connectivity, and show last-updated timestamps whenever live data is unavailable.
